InkDOS advanced tools

Open-source tools that run entirely in your browser. Files are processed on your device and never uploaded.

  • Extract ZIP, RAR and 7zOpen ZIP, RAR and 7z archives and save the files inside.ArchiveDrop · MIT
  • CyberChef (data toolbox)Encode, decode, hash, encrypt, compress and analyse data with hundreds of operations.CyberChef · Apache-2.0
  • IT-Tools (developer utilities)Formatters, converters, generators and network helpers for developers (JSON, YAML, UUID, JWT, regex and more).IT-Tools · GPL-3.0
  • Apple Pages, Numbers and Keynote viewerView .pages, .numbers and .key files in the browser (view only, no conversion).pnk · MIT OR Apache-2.0
  • PDF toolkitMerge, split, compress, convert, OCR, edit and protect PDFs; Word, Excel, PowerPoint and LibreOffice files to PDF, all in the browser.BentoPDF · AGPL-3.0
  • Python terminal (Pyodide)A Python 3 console with numpy, pandas, matplotlib, scipy, openpyxl and more, running entirely in the browser; open and save files.Pyodide · MPL-2.0
  • OpenDocument viewerView LibreOffice / OpenDocument files (.odt, .ods, .odp), view only.WebODF · AGPL-3.0
  • Image converter and compressorConvert images between JPG, PNG, WebP and AVIF, resize them and make them smaller, comparing before and after.Squoosh · Apache-2.0
  • Word document viewerView .docx files with their page layout, headers, footers, tables and images (view only).docx-preview · Apache-2.0
  • PowerPoint viewerView .pptx slides with their layout, shapes, charts and images (view only).pptx-renderer · Apache-2.0
This site only serves files: visitors cannot change them, and only the InkDOS maintainers can publish a new version (security).
